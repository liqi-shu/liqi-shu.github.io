Publications

Selected completed work in stroke prediction, cerebrovascular outcomes, clinical AI, and recovery.

This selection highlights peer-reviewed work with a short statement of what each study contributed. Ongoing projects are not included. For the continuously updated record, visit Google Scholar.

2026

Development and External Validation of an EHR-Derived Model for 30-Day Periprocedural Ischemic Stroke

Shu L, Furie KL, Garcia Guarniz AL, de Havenon A, Khan F, … Wu M, Zheng HB, Yaghi S. Journal of the American Heart Association. PubMed 42261949 · Open calculator · Source code

Developed across 255,850 procedures and externally validated across 189,095 procedures; the locked model achieved an AUC of 0.86 in external validation.

2026

Large Language Models for Accurate Medical Chart Abstraction in Stroke

Zhong Z, Porto CM, Hong D, … Shu L, Baird G, Jayaraman M, Yaghi S, Jiao Z, Wolman DN. American Journal of Neuroradiology. PubMed 41720514

Evaluated 22 open-source language models on 2,416 thrombectomy reports; the best model reached 94.8% overall extraction accuracy.

2025

Perioperative Stroke: Mechanisms, Risk Stratification, and Management

Shu L, Aziz YN, de Havenon A, Messe SR, Nguyen TN, Sur NB, … Yaghi S. Stroke. PubMed 40444359

Synthesizes mechanisms, perioperative risk assessment, prevention, recognition, and management across surgical settings.

2025

Intravenous Thrombolysis in Cervical Artery Dissection–Related Stroke

Shu L, Lee E, Field TS, Guo X, Henninger N, Keser Z, … Yaghi S. Journal of the American Heart Association. PubMed 39968775

In a nationwide analysis, thrombolysis was associated with better odds of home discharge without significantly higher intracerebral hemorrhage or in-hospital death.

2025

Incidence Trends and Risk of Recurrent Stroke of Cervical Artery Dissections in the US

Shu L, Strelecky L, de Havenon A, Nguyen TN, Henninger N, Keser Z, … Yaghi S. Neurology. PubMed 40173376

Characterized US incidence trends and recurrent-stroke risk after cervical artery dissection using national longitudinal data.

2024

Clinical score to predict epilepsy after cerebral venous thrombosis (DIAS³)

Lindgren E, Shu L, Simaan N, … Zuurbier SM. JAMA Neurology. Co-equal contributors. PubMed 39432281 · Open DIAS³ calculator

Developed a six-variable score and externally validated individualized 1- and 3-year epilepsy-risk estimates in two independent cohorts.

2024

Major cardiovascular events and bleeding in atrial fibrillation patients undergoing surgery

Shu L, Jack N, de Havenon A, … Yaghi S. Journal of Stroke and Cerebrovascular Diseases. PubMed 38944363

Used BRIDGE trial data to identify factors associated with perioperative major cardiovascular events and bleeding in patients with atrial fibrillation.

2023

Perioperative Acute Ischemic Stroke in Patients with Atrial Fibrillation

Shu L, Jiang W, Xiao H, Henninger N, Nguyen T, Siegler J, … Yaghi S. Annals of Neurology. PubMed 37183768

Analyzed more than one million elective procedures and developed a perioperative stroke score that outperformed CHA₂DS₂-VASc alone.

2023

Trends in venous thromboembolism readmission after ischemic and hemorrhagic stroke

Shu L, de Havenon A, Liberman AL, Henninger N, … Yaghi S. Journal of Stroke. PubMed 36592970

Across nearly 1.46 million stroke admissions, venous-thromboembolism readmission risk was highest during the first four to six weeks and substantially higher after intracerebral hemorrhage.

2022

Predictors of recurrent venous thrombosis after cerebral venous thrombosis

Shu L, Bakradze E, Omran SS, … Yaghi S. Neurology. PubMed 36123126

Identified clinical factors associated with recurrent venous thrombosis in an international ACTION-CVT cohort.

2022

Direct Oral Anticoagulants Versus Warfarin in Cerebral Venous Thrombosis (ACTION-CVT)

Yaghi S, Shu L, Bakradze E, … Furie K. Stroke. PubMed 35143325

Found similar effectiveness between direct oral anticoagulants and warfarin, with a lower adjusted risk of major hemorrhage in the DOAC group.

2020

Improving Accelerometry-Based Measurement of Functional Upper-Extremity Use After Stroke

Lum PS, Shu L, Bochniewicz EM, Tran T, Chang LC, Barth J, Dromerick AW. Neurorehabilitation and Neural Repair. PubMed 33150830

The best intrasubject machine-learning model detected functional paretic-arm activity with 92.6% accuracy and substantially outperformed the conventional counts-ratio method.

2020

Adversarial active learning for medical concept identification and annotation inconsistency

Yu G, Yang Y, Wang X, … Shu L. Journal of Biomedical Informatics. PubMed 32687985

Used adversarial active learning to improve medical-concept recognition and detect inconsistent annotations in biomedical datasets.
