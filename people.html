Principal investigator

Lab director

Liqi Shu, MD

Vascular Neurologist · UPMC Stroke Institute
Core Faculty · Pitt Cerebrovascular Medicine
Adjunct Instructor in Neurology · Brown University

Dr. Shu combines vascular neurology, neurointerventional training, and computational methods to improve stroke-risk prediction, clinical data extraction, and recovery measurement. His work draws on EHR data, international registries, imaging, EEG, motion capture, and wearable sensors.
